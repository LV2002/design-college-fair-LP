Before you come

Questions people ask

Is there any entry fee?

No. Entry is free for students and parents. We ask you to register only so we can plan seating and send you the final college list in advance.

Do I need to be an AFD India student to attend?

No. The fair is open to any student considering design or architecture, whether or not you study with us.

What should I bring?

Your portfolio if you have one — a sketchbook, printed sheets or work on a laptop or phone all work. Bring a notebook for admissions details, and your parents if they want the careers session.

Can my parents come with me?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. There's a session built specifically for parents on careers, costs and outcomes in design.

I'm in Class 11. Is it too early for me?

It's the best time. Knowing your target colleges in Class 11 lets you shape your portfolio and preparation around them instead of catching up later.

Can our school bring a group?

Yes. Write to info@afdindia.com with the number of students and we'll reserve seats and arrange a briefing slot for your group.
